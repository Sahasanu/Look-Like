## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an editorial luxury experience rooted in mindful indulgence, sensory tranquility, and high-end beauty craftsmanship. Designed for an audience that values artisanal salon services, holistic wellness, and premium editorial aesthetics, the visual world pairs warm, luminous tactile materiality with quiet poise.

The design movement blends **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with classical architectural geometry. The experience avoids cold, clinical whites in favor of radiant ivory, toasted porcelain, and muted blush undertones. Expressive, high-contrast serif typography delivers timeless fashion-magazine polish, while balanced sans-serif controls ensure frictionless digital appointments and transparent service discovery. Archival arch motifs, delicate hairline borders, and warm tinted shadows frame photography and content like gallery exhibits.

## Colors

The palette establishes an opulent, warm-toned sanctuary.

- **Primary (`#C4877A` - Terracotta Rose):** The signature tactile tone used for primary callouts, hero emphasis, active tabs, and refined button accents.
- **Secondary (`#B8925A` - Rose Gold / Warm Ochre):** A metallic-infused heritage accent reserved for badges, hairline dividers, pricing flourishes, and luxury service distinctions.
- **Tertiary (`#2A1F1B` - Deep Espresso):** The primary text color and foundation for dramatic inverted evening panels (such as bridal features and footer spaces), replacing sterile pitch-black with aromatic warmth.
- **Neutral (`#FAF6F2` - Ivory Cream):** The base atmospheric canvas, delivering natural warmth and high visual comfort across editorial spreads.

### Layering & Contrast Rules
- **Base Surfaces:** Pages alternate between `#FAF6F2` for main content and `#FAF8F5` or `#F3E5DD` for cards, form trays, and secondary highlight modules.
- **Dark Mode Blocks:** High-ticket features (e.g., bridal suites, evening consultations) shift deliberately into inverted surfaces anchored by `#2A1F1B` or `#1C1917` with `#FAF6F2` typography and `#C4877A` interactive buttons.
- **Hairline Borders:** Segment dividers and card borders should utilize `#E8DDD5` (or 15% opacity `#2A1F1B`) for delicate structure without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typography pairings contrast the couture sophistication of `Playfair Display` with the crystal-clear readability and warm geometry of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Editorial Emphasis:** Headlines frequently feature an italicized accent keyword (e.g., "Services, *curated*" or "Reserve *your* chair") styled in `subheading` or italicized `headline-lg` using `#C4877A`.
- **Eyebrow Headers:** Section trackers use `label-sm` with wide tracking (`0.14em`), centered, prepended and appended by delicate horizontal rules or diamonds (`◆`).
- **Numerals & Pricing:** Service prices employ `Playfair Display` Medium or Semibold to evoke hand-crafted artisanal ledger aesthetics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes expansive breathing room, deliberate vertical rhythm, and curated asymmetry.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content container width of `1280px`. Breakpoints step down gracefully at `1024px` (8 columns), `768px` (6 columns), and `480px` (4 columns).
- **Section Rhythm:** Major thematic transitions rely on generous spacing (`space-2xl`) to evoke an unhurried, calm salon environment. Internal content clusters maintain an orderly hierarchy with `space-md` between text elements and `space-xl` separating structural groups.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Staggered visual offsets (e.g., floating cards overlapping editorial imagery or archway previews) use controlled `space-lg` negative margins to create layered editorial curiosity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects aggressive drop shadows in favor of ambient warm radiance, soft translucent surfaces, and crisp hairline perimeters.

- **Warm Ambient Bloom:** Modals, floating service cards, and sticky booking bars employ low-opacity espresso-tinted shadows: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -10px rgba(42, 31, 27, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(42, 31, 27, 0.04)`.
- **Architectural Framing:** Image frames and interactive panels use subtle inner hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(196, 135, 122, 0.25)`) to delineate edges cleanly against cream backdrops.
- **Floating Overlays:** Appointment confirmation pills and floating notification badges adopt frosted ivory backgrounds (`rgba(250, 246, 242, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) accented by a warm perimeter stroke.

## Shapes

The design system uses a base roundedness factor of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) for standard controls, while introducing bespoke architectural curvatures for signature components:

- **Arch Cards (`rounded-arch`):** Curated images, artist showcases, and signature experience highlights use semi-circular vaulted tops (`border-radius: 9999px 9999px 0.5rem 0.5rem`).
- **Pill Elements:** Interactive actions, primary CTAs, and category filtering tabs utilize fully rounded caps (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Form Controls & Trays:** Input fields, service item cards, and booking date matrices use standardized `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`) to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) corners for effortless modern utility.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** `#2A1F1B` background, `#FAF6F2` text, fully rounded (`9999px`), padding `0.875rem 2rem`. On hover, transitions smoothly to `#C4877A`.
- **Rose Accent Button:** Solid `#C4877A` background with crisp white or cream text, utilized for high-priority calls-to-action (e.g., "Book Appointment").
- **Secondary Ghost / Outline:** Transparent background, `1px solid #2A1F1B` border, `#2A1F1B` text. On hover, fills with `#F3E5DD`.
- **Floating Booking Bar:** Sticky mobile/desktop instant action button styled in energetic salon-supportive `#25D366` or brand `#C4877A`.

### Category Chips & Filters
- **Pill Tabs:** Horizontal scrollable cluster. Default inactive state uses `#FAF8F5` surface with `1px solid #E8DDD5` and `#2A1F1B` text. Active state adopts solid `#2A1F1B` with `#FAF6F2` text.

### Cards & Arched Containers
- **Service Arch Card:** Features an arched image canopy (`border-radius: 140px 140px 12px 12px`), followed by centered Playfair Display title, italic service note, and an unobtrusive price tag.
- **Feature Review Badge:** Positioned over hero imagery using a frosted ivory capsule (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.94)`), subtle shadow, small circular staff avatar, and tiny uppercase metadata.

### Lists & Pricing Menus
- **Menu Row Item:** Clean tabular treatment with left-aligned treatment name and brief subtext, separated by an optional dot leader or subtle bottom border (`#E8DDD5`), concluding with right-aligned currency in `Playfair Display`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Background `#FAF8F5` or pure white, `1px solid #E8DDD5`, rounded `0.5rem`, padding `0.875rem 1rem`. Active focus shifts border to `#C4877A` with a subtle `0 0 0 3px rgba(196, 135, 122, 0.15)` ring.
- **Select Dropdown:** Warm ivory chevron icon indicator, matching typography and spacing tokens.
